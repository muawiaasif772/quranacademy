Noorani Qaida for Beginners

Our Noorani Qaida course is designed to build a strong foundation for reading the Quran. We focus on letter recognition, correct pronunciation (Makharij), and basic joining of letters using the proven Noorani Qaida methodology. Students learn to recognize the Arabic alphabet in its various forms and master the basic rules of phonetic articulation. Lessons are live and one-to-one, and start with a free trial so your tutor can check your level first. Most beginners move from letters to reading short words within the first few weeks, then build up to full verses at their own pace.
